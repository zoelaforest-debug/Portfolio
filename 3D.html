About the collection

All these images are related to different types of 3D projects. The LPS Fox was made using a 3D printer and taught us how to use the printer. The Blender Desk was made in Blender and showed us the basics of creating in that application. The desk scene colors initially had no correlation, but after a while I started to imagine Dora the Explorer living there. The Virtual Space one allowed us to create art that we are able to virtually walk through. I made mine to represent my love for marine biology and fantasy.
