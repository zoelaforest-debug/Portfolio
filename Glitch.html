Glitch

About the collection

These pieces were part of an assignment called "Glitch Art". We took regular undisturbed images and ran through several programs to create the glitched images shown above. For "29 remixed" and "29 remixed 2.0", they are "29" glitched even further. The assignment was a lot of fun but took a really long time to accomplish for certain pieces. The piece titled "29" is titles this way because that's the amount of attempts it took to accomplish that look./p>
